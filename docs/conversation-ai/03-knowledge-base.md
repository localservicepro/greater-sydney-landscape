Daniel gives a fixed price after seeing the job. These things affect it:

## 5. Services

### 5.1 Lawn Mowing

Regular lawn mowing across Blacktown, Toongabbie, Seven Hills, Parramatta and The Hills. You pick the schedule and we keep to it.

**Every visit includes:**

- The mow.
- Edging along paths, driveways and garden beds.
- Whipper-snipping around fences, trees and posts.
- Paths and driveways blown clean.
- Clippings removed and taken away, or mulched if you prefer.
- Nature strips and front verges mown on request, so the whole frontage looks the same.

**Schedules:** fortnightly, three-weekly or monthly. Most customers are on a fortnightly round through spring and summer, then three-weekly or monthly in autumn and winter when growth slows. Daniel recommends a schedule when quoting and adjusts it through the year.

**Lawn types:** buffalo, couch and kikuyu lawns are the most common in Sydney.

### 5.2 Ride-On Mowing

Part of the lawn mowing service, for larger blocks.

- Ride-on mowers for properties over about 800 square metres.
- Suits acreage, paddocks, big backyards, corner blocks, nature strips, strata common areas, church grounds, sports fields and rural-residential blocks.
- Common in Dural, Kenthurst, Glenorie, The Hills and Hornsby.
- Edges and tight corners are finished with a push mower and whipper-snipper, so the whole property looks consistent.
- The job is done in a fraction of the time and priced accordingly.

### 5.3 Hedge Trimming

Hedge trimming for homes in The Hills, Hornsby, Dural, Parramatta and the Inner West.

**Includes:**

- Formal hedge shaping with string lines and levels, for straight faces and a level top.
- Height reduction on overgrown screens.
- Side trimming along fences and driveways.
- Boundary screens and privacy hedges.
- Pole hedgers and platforms for taller screens.
- All clippings removed from site, and paths and driveways blown down.

**Common hedge plants:** lilly pilly, murraya, photinia, viburnum, buxus and leighton green.

**How often:** fast growers like lilly pilly and murraya look best trimmed three to four times a year. Slower formal hedges such as buxus usually need two to three trims a year.

**Overgrown hedges:** most can be brought down in one or two stages, depending on the species and how far back it needs to go. Daniel will say honestly if a hedge is too far gone to recover.

### 5.4 Garden Maintenance

Scheduled garden upkeep for homes, strata and commercial grounds across Parramatta, The Hills, Blacktown and the Inner West.

**A typical visit includes:**

- Pruning and shaping shrubs and small trees.
- Weeding garden beds and paved areas.
- Edging and a general tidy-up.
- Mulching and bed top-ups.
- Seasonal tidy-ups and leaf removal.
- All green waste removed.

Hedge trimming, lawn mowing and fertilising can be added to the same visit, so one crew handles the whole property on one invoice.

**How often:** most established gardens stay in good shape with a monthly visit, with fortnightly visits through spring and summer when growth is fastest.

**One-off clean-ups:** yes. If a garden has got away from you, we can do a one-off tidy-up and then move you onto a regular schedule.

**Strata and body corporate:** common-area gardens, nature strips, entry plantings and lawns for apartment blocks and townhouse complexes around Parramatta, Westmead, Blacktown and The Hills, on a fixed fortnightly or monthly schedule with one monthly invoice. Pressure washing of common paths and driveways can be added to the same contract.

**Commercial grounds:** offices, medical centres, childcare centres, churches and retail sites across Western Sydney. Frontages kept presentable, paths and car parks clear of weeds and leaf litter, garden beds mulched. We are happy to work to a site induction or specific access hours.

### 5.5 Weed Spraying

Part of the lawn care service.

- Bindis in spring, clover and broadleaf weeds through summer, and winter grass once the weather cools.
- Registered selective herbicides that target the weeds and leave the lawn, applied by a trained operator and timed to catch weeds before they seed.
- A separate knockdown treatment for paths, driveways, gravel areas and fence lines.
- **Bindis:** treat in late winter and early spring before they set seed. Two treatments a few weeks apart usually clears them for the season.
- **Kids and pets:** we tell you how long to keep off the lawn, typically until the spray has dried, and we tell you the product used on every visit.

### 5.6 Plant and Lawn Fertilising

Part of the lawn care service.

- Slow-release lawn fertiliser in early spring and again in early autumn, with a lighter feed in mid-summer if the lawn is under heat stress.
- Applications are timed around rain so nothing is wasted.
- Plant fertilising for garden beds, hedges, natives and fruit trees on the same visit, so the whole property greens up together.
- Advice on watering and mowing height.
- After each lawn care visit you get a short note saying what went down and when to water.
- Lawn care can be bundled into a regular mowing round, or booked on its own.

### 5.7 Pressure Washing

High pressure cleaning for homeowners, landlords, real estate agents and strata managers across Parramatta, Blacktown, The Hills, Toongabbie and the Inner West.

**Surfaces:**

- Concrete and exposed-aggregate driveways.
- Pavers, patios, alfresco areas and pool surrounds.
- Paths, steps and retaining walls.
- Rendered walls, brickwork, Colorbond and timber fences, and outdoor furniture.
- Strata common areas: paths, driveways, car parks, bin areas, stairwells and entries.

**How we do it:** commercial pressure washers and surface cleaners that lift dirt, tyre marks, oil, moss and mould without streaking. Moss and mould are pre-treated so they do not come straight back. Pressure is adjusted for pavers, stone, render and timber so nothing is damaged. Loose sand between pavers can be re-sanded afterwards if needed.

**Timing:** a standard double driveway takes one to two hours including pre-treatment. Most driveway, path and patio jobs are done in a morning. Daniel gives a time estimate with the quote.

**Common reasons:** pre-sale and end-of-lease clean-ups, preparing for a repaint or new landscaping, and scheduled strata cleaning. One-off or scheduled.

### 5.8 Landscaping Projects

Landscaping across Parramatta and Western Sydney, from design through to the last barrow of mulch.

**Includes:**

- Garden design and planting plans.
- New lawns: soil preparation, then supplying and laying buffalo, couch or kikuyu turf to suit the site, or a seeded lawn.
- Garden beds with edging, and mulching.
- Native and low-maintenance planting, and hedges for privacy.
- Front yard and street-appeal makeovers.
- Ongoing maintenance once the project is finished.

**New builds:** bare-block landscaping for new homes in estates around Blacktown, Marsden Park, Kellyville and The Ponds is a large part of our project work, usually delivered in one or two stages. For renovations we work around the builders.

**Timing:** a front yard makeover typically takes two to four days. Larger new-build gardens run one to two weeks depending on size and materials. Daniel gives a timeline with the quote.

**Plant choice:** hardy natives, well-chosen hedging species and lawns suited to the aspect, picked to cope with Western Sydney clay and heat and to be realistic to maintain.
